# Roadmap

Ohara stays focused on a few core features. This page lists what is planned next. Releases and their notes are on [GitHub](https://github.com/ntrossat/ohara/releases).

## Planned

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| AI chat over the docs | Ask questions on the website and get answers grounded in the docs, with links to the pages they come from |

## Suggest a feature

Open a [feature request](https://github.com/ntrossat/ohara/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml). Agree on the idea there before writing the code: see [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/ntrossat/ohara/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
